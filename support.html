Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't covered here, leave feedback via the app's App Store listing and we'll follow up in the next update.

Why do I need to sign in with Apple?

Your first question is always free to answer, no account needed. Starting with the second question, signing in with Apple keeps everyone's vote counted exactly once — without it, someone could reinstall the app and vote again, which would skew the crowd stats for everyone else.

I just installed the app and don't see any ads — is that normal?

Yes. Newly created ad placements can take up to an hour to start serving. The app shows a blank banner slot in the meantime rather than an error.

How is the "crowd majority" calculated?

It's a live tally of every signed-in vote submitted for that question, updated in real time as more people play.

How do I delete my data?

See the Privacy Policy for what's collected and how to request deletion.

I found a factual error in a question — how do I report it?

Use the "Submit Effect Experience" form in the app's Settings tab, or leave a review on the App Store — both are reviewed regularly.
